Soft Glass

Soft Glass. Off-white canvas, each node a small white card with a thin grey border and two layers of subtle shadow (1px tight + 16px soft). A tiny accent dot in the corner colour-codes the type; the label and meta line carry the information. Selected nodes get a 1.5px indigo border. Reads as restrained, modern enterprise (Linear / Notion). Best when you want the typography to do the work and the chrome to recede.
