Refined Cards

Refined Cards. Cool-grey canvas, each node a structured card: 3-px coloured accent bar across the top (encodes type), grey eyebrow header, bold title, mono meta line, and a tag pill at the bottom. Selected nodes get a 1.5-px blue border + a soft 3-px blue ring. Reads as a Stripe / Linear / Notion enterprise dashboard. Densest of the five — every node carries 3-4 lines of context, so the chart is also a list. Best when reviewers need to scan rich metadata at a glance without clicking anything.
