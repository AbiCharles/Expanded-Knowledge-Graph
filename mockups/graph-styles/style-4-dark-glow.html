Dark Glow

Dark Glow. Near-black canvas with a faint cyan grid; each node a thin-bordered dark rectangle with a 6+14 px cyan glow. Selected nodes step up to a brighter cyan + stronger glow; the Outcome uses an amber border so the resolution stands out. Edges are 1.1 px cyan-tinted lines with their own subtle glow. Pure DM Mono typography. Reads as a SOC dashboard / threat-intel console (Palantir Gotham, Recorded Future). Best for graph views the reviewer leans into during a live demo — bold, cinematic.
