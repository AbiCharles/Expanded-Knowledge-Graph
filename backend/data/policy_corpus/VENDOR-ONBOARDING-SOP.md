# SOP-PP-ONBOARD-002 — Vendor Onboarding

## Purpose
Define the gates a candidate vendor must clear before being authorized as an active supplier.

## Required diligence
- **D&B / DUNS verification** — confirms legal existence, country of registration, ownership disclosure
- **Financial risk score** — D&B PAYDEX, current ratio, cash position. Score must be at or above 65/100 for tier-1 suppliers
- **ESG score** — must be at or above 65/100; verifiable carbon-neutral commitment for any new tier-1 supplier
- **Quality system documentation** — ISO 9001 audit report no older than 24 months
- **Reference customer interviews** — at least three current accounts, ideally with comparable spend levels
- **Manufacturing capability audit** — for tier-1 suppliers in manufacturing categories, an on-site audit (or qualified third-party audit) within the last 12 months

## Approval matrix
| Tier | Annual spend | Required approver |
|------|--------------|-------------------|
| Tier 3 | < $250k | Buyer |
| Tier 2 | $250k–$1M | Procurement category manager |
| Tier 1 | > $1M | Procurement category manager + procurement director (two-person rule) |

## Initial volume cap
New tier-1 suppliers are capped at 15% of category volume for the first six months. Performance review triggers re-evaluation at month 6.

## Audit
The complete onboarding package and all reviewers' decisions must be retained in the audit store for the contract lifetime plus seven years.
