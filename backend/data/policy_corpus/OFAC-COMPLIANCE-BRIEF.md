# OFAC Compliance Brief — Sanctioned Counterparty Engagement

## Background
The Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) maintains the Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) and Blocked Persons List. US persons are prohibited from transacting with listed entities and individuals without a specific or general license.

## Applicability to procurement and trade
Any inbound or outbound transaction involving a counterparty on the SDN List requires either:
- a **specific license** issued by OFAC for that exact transaction, or
- an applicable **general license** under the relevant sanctions program (e.g. agriculture, medicine, telecommunications)

## License verification
Operator-provided license claims are not sufficient. The compliance officer must:
1. Cross-reference the license number against OFAC's licensing database
2. Confirm the license covers the specific product, quantity, and counterparty
3. Verify the license is active and not nearing expiration
4. Document the verification step in the case file

## Key sanctions programs (as of Q2 2026)
- **IRAN-EO13599** — broad blocking of Iranian government and SDN-listed entities
- **RUSSIA-EO14024** — Russia-related sanctions covering finance, defence, and oligarchs
- **DPRK** — comprehensive North Korea sanctions
- **VENEZUELA** — selected Venezuelan officials and PDVSA
- **SDGT** — Specially Designated Global Terrorists
- **SYRIA** — Syrian government and aligned entities

## Penalty framework
Civil penalties up to USD 1.5M per violation; criminal penalties up to USD 1M and 20 years per intentional violation.
